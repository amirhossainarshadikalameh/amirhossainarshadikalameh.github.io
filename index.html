Research projects

Complex networks & statistical physics

Complexity of Real and Model Networks B.Sc. thesis

Feb 2025 – Present

Supervisor: Prof. Farhad Shahbazi · Isfahan University of Technology · with M. Tabatabaei

  • Compared enzyme, metabolic and protein–protein interaction networks with Erdős–Rényi, Barabási–Albert and Watts–Strogatz models using Laplacian spectra and multi-centrality statistics: low-rewiring small-world models fit enzyme and metabolic networks best, while protein interaction networks are significantly more complex.
  • Null-model test (10 ENZYMES networks, 20 centralities, configuration-model ensembles, Stouffer and Wilcoxon statistics): centrality entropy is largely fixed by the degree sequence, unlike structural descriptors (σcorr, clustering, modularity).
  • Now extending the framework with the conditional entropy S(c | k) of centralities given degree.

OutputB.Sc. thesis (2026) · manuscript in preparation [1]

Correlations Between Centrality Measures

Nov 2024 – Present

Supervisor: Prof. Farhad Shahbazi · Isfahan University of Technology · with M. Tabatabaei

  • Mapped correlations among 11 centrality measures across ER, BA and WS ensembles, identifying positive, negative and neutral correlation regimes.
  • Proposed the Shannon entropy of centrality distributions as a quantitative indicator of network complexity.
  • Real enzyme networks follow small-world correlation structure: WS networks with p = 0.01 give the best match in 9 of 10 cases.

OutputPoster, ICTP Spring College in the Physics of Complex Systems, 2026 [3]

Predicting Algebraic Connectivity with Graph Neural Networks

2026

With Yasin Jahangiri · Isfahan University of Technology

  • Generated 2,000 graphs per ensemble (BA, ER, WS) with 11 low-cost structural features, linked them into a k-nearest-neighbour “graph of graphs” and trained a two-layer graph convolutional network to predict the Laplacian spectral gap λ2.
  • Reached ≈ 95% accuracy on held-out graphs, a fast surrogate for eigen-decomposition across large network ensembles.

OutputManuscript in preparation [2]

Time Series as Complex Networks Ongoing

Jun 2026 – Present
