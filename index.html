I am a physics graduate of Isfahan University of Technology, where I ranked first in my department for four years and wrote my B.Sc. thesis on the complexity of real and model networks with Prof. Farhad Shahbazi. My work sits where network science, statistical physics and quantitative biology meet.

I test when network-complexity measures carry information beyond null models, build machine-learning surrogates for spectral properties of graphs, and model metabolic regulation in living systems. I have worked at the Max Planck School Matter to Life in Dresden and in the Quantitative Life Sciences section of ICTP in Trieste.

Open to fully funded master’s and PhD positions in complex systems, network science and statistical physics — with applications to biological, financial and other real-world systems. Get in touch
